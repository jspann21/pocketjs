POCKETJS IPOD PHOTO - CAMPAIGN 4 AUDIO HARDWARE GATE

This candidate is NOT hardware-qualified. It tests a short left/right tone,
not music streaming. Keep the iPod charged. Use ordinary wired headphones,
not an amplifier or dock line-out. Start with the earpieces away from your
ears; disconnect them immediately if output is loud or abnormal. Volume is
fixed low in the firmware; wheel motion does not change it.

1. With the restored iPod in disk mode at F:\, open PowerShell in this folder:

   Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process Bypass
   .\INSTALL.ps1

2. Safely eject, disconnect USB, and boot. The normal app should appear with
   PWR BAT after the source settles. AUD READY may appear briefly first; you
   do not need to catch it. There must be no continuous sound at boot.

3. Connect headphones. Hold Left+Right together for one second, with no other
   button held. Expect AUD TONE briefly, then AUD OFF. The intended sound is
   a quiet 440 Hz tone in LEFT only for 1/4 second, 1/10 second of silence,
   then RIGHT only for 1/4 second. The app can pause during this 0.6-second
   diagnostic. Release both buttons completely. If the sound is normal and
   quiet, listen normally and repeat once. Record the channel order, sound,
   final label, and any pop/noise. If silent, report silence; do not amplify it.

4. On battery, hold Menu+Right for two seconds, release fully, then press one
   button to wake. Expect the screen to return and RESUME. Repeat Left+Right
   once; the same left/right sequence and AUD OFF must return. AUD OFF and
   RESUME are last-event labels and may remain until another event. The
   existing 10-second idle suspend remains active.

5. Connect USB and wait for PWR USB. Repeat Left+Right once. Verify the same
   tone and no lost display. Do not repeat unrelated cable/battery campaigns.

6. Press center once for LCD WAKE, then Play once for SHDN READY. Hold
   Menu+Play for two seconds to return to disk mode. Once F:\ mounts, run:

   .\RESTORE.ps1

7. Safely eject and confirm normal Rockbox boot. Return RESULTS.txt along
   with INSTALL-STATUS.json and RESTORE-STATUS.json.

STOP on AUD E##, KERN E##, loud/continuous noise, lost display, disk error,
or unexpected reboot. AUD E02 = bounded I2C failure; E03 = tone clock timing
outside the expected range; E04 = FIFO timeout. Do not keep retrying a fault.
If necessary use Menu+Select to hard-reset, then Select+Play for boot-ROM
disk mode and run RESTORE.ps1. These are recovery actions, not test passes.
Do not install directly into OSOS or alter the firmware partition.
