POCKETJS IPOD PHOTO - RUNTIME RELIABILITY BATCH

This is one install and three boots. Do not reconnect between boots.

1. Connect the restored iPod in disk mode. Confirm it is F:\.
2. Open PowerShell in this folder and run:

   Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process Bypass
   .\INSTALL.ps1

3. Safely eject and boot. BETA should launch. Record the top-right line.
4. Press center once. The screen should recover to ALPHA without rebooting.
   Record the new top-right line and confirm the wheel still works.
5. Hold Menu+Play for two seconds. ALPHA should launch after reboot. Record
   the top-right line.
6. Hold Menu+Play for two seconds again. ALPHA should launch. Record the line.
7. Reconnect in disk mode and run:

   .\RESTORE.ps1

8. Safely eject and confirm normal Rockbox boot.

Expected lines:

   SAFE A G00003
   RECV E G00004
   ROLL L G00006
   KEEP L G00009

Any LINE E## line, MEMG panic, missing app, or different generation is a
failure. Reconnect in disk mode and run .\RESTORE.ps1. Do not repeat earlier
rectangle, heartbeat, Hold, power, launcher, ordinary-app, or persistence
observations.

Return only the four lines, whether recovery remained responsive, whether
restore verified, whether Rockbox booted, and anything unexpected. Send a
photo only if a line is missing or unreadable.
