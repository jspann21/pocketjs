POCKETJS IPOD PHOTO - NATIVE PCM STREAMING BATCH, REVISION 2

New candidate, NOT hardware-qualified. The passed short-tone kit is unchanged.
This revision fixes queue-demand refilling and adds producer service between
guest frames and during display transfers. The previous early STR E06 remains
a failure; this revision still enforces the same underrun checks.
Use a charged iPod and ordinary wired headphones, not an amplifier or dock
line-out. Start with earpieces away from your ears; disconnect immediately if
sound is loud or abnormal. Volume is fixed low; the wheel does not change it.

1. In this folder, with the restored iPod in disk mode at F:\, run:

   Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process Bypass
   .\INSTALL.ps1

2. Safely eject, disconnect USB, and boot. The normal app and PWR BAT should
   appear. Connect headphones. Hold Left+Right together for one second, with
   all other buttons released, then release both completely.

3. Let the approximately eleven-second batch finish. Expect quiet notes
   alternating LEFT, RIGHT, then BOTH ears, and these labels:

   STR PLAY -> STR PAUSE (one second) -> STR PLAY -> STR GAP (one second)
   -> STR PLAY -> STR DRAIN -> STR PASS

   The gap is intentional: the producer stops, the queued sound finishes,
   then silence is output until feeding resumes. STR DRAIN can be brief.
   During STR PLAY, move the wheel and verify the screen/app still responds.
   Do not press center or Play during this batch: those retain the LCD and
   shutdown-preflight controls. Note whether startup crackle is better,
   unchanged, or worse, and any clicks on pause, resume, or stop.

4. Start again with Left+Right. While playing, release and hold Left+Right
   again for one second. Expect STR STOP and silence. Release fully. Start
   once more and let it reach STR PASS. This checks cancel/flush/restart.

5. Start again on battery; during playback hold Menu+Right for two seconds.
   The screen should suspend and audio stop. Release both, then press one
   button once to wake. Expect RESUME, with no automatic sound. Start the
   batch again and let it reach STR PASS.

6. Connect USB, wait for PWR USB, and run one complete batch to STR PASS.
   Then press center for LCD WAKE and Play for SHDN READY. Hold Menu+Play for
   two seconds to enter disk mode. When F:\ mounts, run:

   .\RESTORE.ps1

7. Safely eject and confirm original Rockbox boots. Return RESULTS.txt,
   INSTALL-STATUS.json, and RESTORE-STATUS.json.

STOP on STR E##, AUD E##, KERN E##, continuous/loud noise, frozen display,
unexpected reboot, or disk error. Record the exact label; do not repeatedly
retry a fault. STR E01 is codec control, E06 unexpected underrun count,
E07 retirement/progress, E09 pause preservation; E10 and above are DMA-driver
faults. For recovery only: Menu+Select hard reset, then Select+Play boot-ROM
disk mode, followed by RESTORE.ps1. Never write the firmware partition/OSOS.
