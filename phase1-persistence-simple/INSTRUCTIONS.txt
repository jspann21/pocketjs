POCKETJS IPOD PHOTO - CONFINED PERSISTENCE GATE

This is one install and three boots. Do not reconnect between boots.

1. Restore Rockbox first if an older PocketJS test is installed.
2. Connect the iPod in disk mode. Confirm it is F:\ (or pass another drive).
3. Open PowerShell in this folder and run:

   Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process Bypass
   .\INSTALL.ps1

4. Safely eject and boot. Record the single top-right PERS line.
5. Press center once. Record the new PERS line.
6. Hold Menu+Play for two seconds to reboot. Record the PERS line.
7. Press Play once by itself. Record the new PERS line.
8. Hold Menu+Play for two seconds to reboot. Record the final PERS line.
9. Reconnect in disk mode and run:

   .\RESTORE.ps1

10. Safely eject and confirm normal Rockbox boot.

Expected lines, in order:

   PERS L0 G00001
   PERS C1 G00002
   PERS L1 G00002
   PERS A0 G00003
   PERS L1 G00002

Any PERS E## line is a failure. Stop testing, reconnect in disk mode, and run
.\RESTORE.ps1. Do not repeat prior launcher, rectangle, heartbeat, Hold, power,
or ordinary-app observations.

Return RESULTS.txt, INSTALL-STATUS.json, and RESTORE-STATUS.json. Send one photo
only if a line is missing or unreadable.
