POCKETJS IPOD PHOTO - PACKAGE LINEAGE AND ROLLBACK GATE

This is one install and three boots. Do not reconnect between boots.

1. Restore Rockbox first if an older PocketJS test is installed.
2. Connect the iPod in disk mode. Confirm it is F:\ (or pass another drive).
3. Open PowerShell in this folder and run:

   Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process Bypass
   .\INSTALL.ps1

4. Safely eject and boot. Confirm BETA launches and record the top-right line.
5. Press center once. Wait for and record the new top-right line.
6. Hold Menu+Play for two seconds to reboot. Confirm ALPHA launches and record
   the top-right line.
7. Hold Menu+Play for two seconds again. Confirm ALPHA launches and record the
   final top-right line.
8. Reconnect in disk mode and run:

   .\RESTORE.ps1

9. Safely eject and confirm normal Rockbox boot.

Expected lines, in order:

   ACPT P G00003
   CRSH P G00004
   ROLL A G00006
   KEEP A G00009

Any LINE E## line, missing app, or different generation is a failure. Stop,
reconnect in disk mode, and run .\RESTORE.ps1. Do not repeat earlier launcher,
rectangle, heartbeat, Hold, power, ordinary-app, or persistence observations.

Return RESULTS.txt, INSTALL-STATUS.json, and RESTORE-STATUS.json. Send one photo
only if a line is missing or unreadable.
